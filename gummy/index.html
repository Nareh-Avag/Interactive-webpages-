Material study / No. 02 / Soft body

Gummy Bear.

One gelatin bear, simulated as a lattice of particles and rendered as translucent candy. Drag it to stretch, let go to fling it, click the floor to drop it.

The bear is a blobby isosurface skinned to a coarse lattice of particles. Springs along the lattice edges and diagonals resist stretch and shear; tetrahedral volume constraints keep it from going flat. Light enters, attenuates through the thick parts, and leaves.
