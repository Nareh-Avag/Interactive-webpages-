Inside the experiment

The crocodile is a signed distance field: a boxy superellipse section swept along a curved spine, with capsule legs, a crest of triangular plates, keeled scutes and an upper head swung open about the corner of the mouth. Surface nets turn it into two meshes, the clear jelly and a cream core.

Underneath, a hex lattice split into five tetrahedra per cell is simulated with XPBD shape matching at 60 Hz, ten substeps per frame. Muscles are rest shapes rewritten each frame: the jaw hinges about the mouth corner and a travelling wave bends the spine. Rendering is a shadow map, a back-face thickness pass and refraction through the front faces with Beer–Lambert absorption.
