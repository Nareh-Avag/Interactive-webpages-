Jelly Tank.

A tank of soft-bodied jellyfish, simulated as springs and chains, then rendered as nothing but type. Stir the water with the cursor. Click to add one, drag to catch one.

Each bell is a ring of twenty-four points held by springs. A pulse squeezes the ring and pushes the body up; tentacles hang as chains that only ever follow. The grid samples the body's density and picks a character for every cell.

About the experiment

Jelly Tank.

A small study in making characters feel wet. Every frame, a field of soft bodies is sampled onto a monospace grid and each cell is given one glyph from a ramp. There are no sprites and no images. The bell is a ring of points on springs with a non-sinusoidal pulse; the tentacles are Verlet chains dragged through a coarse velocity field that you stir with the cursor.

Built as a single HTML file with no dependencies beyond two typefaces.
